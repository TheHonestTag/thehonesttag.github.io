Privacy statement

Who is responsible

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). When you open a page, the host processes technical data such as your IP address, the time and the page requested, and keeps it in server logs. We have no access to those logs. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in running the site securely (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The provider may process data in the USA under its own terms and safeguards.

Fonts and scripts are loaded from this site itself, so your browser contacts no other party just to display the pages.

Browser storage on your device

To remember your language, your garment, your saved scenarios and your wardrobe, the site stores a few items in your browser (see the cookie policy for the names). They stay on your device, are not sent to us and are needed for the function you asked for (§ 25(2) TDDDG). You can delete them in your browser settings.

Analytics (only with your consent)

If you click “Accept all” in the banner, we use Google Analytics 4 (Google Ireland Ltd. / Google LLC) to count visits and see which options people use. Cookies are set and data is sent to Google only after you consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 25(1) TDDDG). You can withdraw at any time through the Cookie preferences button. Details: cookie policy.

Contact by email

If you write to the email address above, we use your email address and message only to reply (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) and delete them when the matter is finished. Mail to this address is forwarded by Cloudflare Email Routing (Cloudflare, Inc., USA).

Your rights

You have the right to access, correction, deletion, restriction, data portability and to object, and to withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future. You can also complain to a data protection authority; for Hesse this is the Hessischer Beauftragter für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit (HBDI), Wiesbaden. Use the contact details above to exercise your rights.
