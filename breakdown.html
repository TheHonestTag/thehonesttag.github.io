02

Phase 2

Inventory analysis

The process tree of one life cycle. Each box is a process; lines carry its output to the next process, drawn thicker the more it carries. Click any box to see its inputs and outputs.

Life cycle inventory

Open a stage to see its flows

Amounts are per functional unit. Hangnote's factors already combine CO₂, methane and nitrous oxide into CO₂e, so the inventory reports greenhouse gases as CO₂e for each process. A full inventory would list each gas separately. Model is a value straight from the Methodology tables, Derived is calculated from those values, and Assumption is an extra assumption used only to show the amount.
